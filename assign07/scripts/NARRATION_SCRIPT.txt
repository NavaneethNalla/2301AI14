[STEP 2: ARCHITECTURE & FORMAL STATE MACHINE OVERVIEW]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMAND TO RUN:
(Display contract structure summary)

SPOKEN NARRATION:
"Before deploying, let us review the security architecture:
1. State Machine: The contract begins in the 'Created' state. Once the buyer deposits exact target funds, it transitions to 'Funded'. From 'Funded', the buyer can release payment to 'Released', or either party can raise a dispute transitioning to 'Disputed'. In a dispute, the arbiter resolves the funds either to the buyer or to the seller.
2. Checks-Effects-Interactions (CEI) & Pull Payments: To prevent reentrancy and denial-of-service, funds are never pushed directly in state transitions. Instead, the contract credits an internal balance ledger, and beneficiaries execute a dedicated 'withdrawFunds' pull-payment transaction.
3. Access Control: Zero address checks and strict role separation prevent address collision."
