## 2. Video Recording Checklist

1. **Recording Software**:
   - **Windows Game Bar** (`Win + G` $\to$ Record) or **OBS Studio** (recommended, record display or window).
2. **Audio Setup**:
   - Ensure your microphone is enabled and clearly audible.
3. **Execution Flow**:
   - When you start `demo.bat`, the runner will display the **exact sentence to read aloud** in bright cyan text!
   - Read the sentence into your microphone.
   - Press **`[ENTER]`** on your keyboard.
   - The runner executes the command automatically, prints the live output, and moves to the next sentence!
4. **YouTube Upload**:
   - Upload each recording as an **Unlisted** video on YouTube with title format:  
     `2301AI14 - Assignment X Demo - Navaneeth Nalla`
   - Copy the link and submit to the official [MS Form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/huBck9hkD7).
